What I design

Flight hardware, explained simply

  • Power unit PCDU

    The satellite’s power plant. It takes energy from the solar panels and battery and delivers it safely to every system on board.

  • Magnetometer unit

    A compass for orbit. It measures Earth’s magnetic field so the satellite knows which way it is facing.

  • Inertial unit IMU

    Senses every turn and wobble, so the satellite can hold steady and point where it needs to.

  • Analog data unit ADHU

    Reads very faint sensor signals with ultra-low noise. My design improved how accurately the satellite knows its orientation.
